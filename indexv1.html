Based in Gainesville, Virginia, Malik Watches is an independent workshop operated by Irfan Malik. Combining a rigorous, data-driven approach to mechanical diagnostics with traditional horological techniques, every project is handled with exacting precision. From complete teardowns of vintage calibers to building custom hybrid assemblies and utilizing specialized 3D-printed bench tools, the focus is always on uncompromising quality and reliable performance.
