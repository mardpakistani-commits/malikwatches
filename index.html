How It Works

The Process

Step 01Intake & Quote

Submit your watch details and requested service. You'll receive a preliminary cost breakdown and insured shipping instructions within 48 hours.

Step 02Diagnostic & Approval

Your timepiece undergoes a full bench inspection under magnification and on the timegrapher. Work begins only after you approve the itemized parts list.

Step 03Bench Work

Disassembly, ultrasonic cleaning, lubrication to manufacturer spec, and reassembly — photographed at each stage so you can see exactly what was done.

Step 04Testing & Return

Multi-position regulation, 48-hour power-reserve observation, and pressure testing before your watch is securely packed and shipped back, fully insured.
